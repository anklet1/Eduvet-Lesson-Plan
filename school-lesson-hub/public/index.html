Core Capabilities

Everything Teachers Need. Nothing They Don't.

No complicated setups or fragile cloud dependencies. EduVet is purpose-built for Ghanaian school classrooms.

NaCCA 3-Phase Notes

Generate official Phase 1 (Starter), Phase 2 (Main), and Phase 3 (Reflection) lesson plans aligned to the NaCCA curriculum standards for Basic 1 to 9.

GES 50/50 SBA Grading

Automatic computation of Homework (15%), Projects (15%), Class Tests (20%), and 50% Exam with instant WAEC Stanine 1–9 and BECE Best 6 aggregates.

100% Offline LAN Sync

Operates completely offline. Teachers connect over the school's local offline Wi-Fi without needing internet access, bundles, or airtime.

SISO Reviewer & Stamp

Headteachers and Circuit Supervisors (SISO) vet weekly notes, apply official SVG security rubber stamps, and track classroom compliance.

Zero Internet Architecture

Built for Schools Where Internet Connectivity is Unreliable.

Most rural and suburban schools face poor data connectivity. EduVet operates directly from the device's local memory and IndexedDB database. Teachers never wait for a page to load or risk losing notes because the network went down.
