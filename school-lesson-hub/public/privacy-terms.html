About the Application & Developer

Eduvet School Hub ("Application" or "Service") is developed and maintained by Edubyte Africa Inc. ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"), an educational technology company registered in Accra, Ghana. Official business inquiries, policy questions, and correspondence may be directed to our official business email: info@edubyteafrica.com. The Application is designed exclusively as an institutional curriculum management, lesson planning, and assessment recording tool for teachers, headteachers, and circuit supervisors.

Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly necessary to provide the services requested:

  • Account & Educator Profile Data: Name, professional email address, telephone number, official Staff/Teacher ID, registered school name, district/circuit, assigned grade levels, and assigned subject curricula.
  • User-Generated Educational Content: Termly schemes of learning, daily/weekly lesson notes, teaching learning materials (TLMs), continuous assessment records (SBA), and student performance summaries created by teachers.
  • Institutional Review & Vetting Metadata: Headteacher verification stamps, inspection feedback notes, and submission timestamps.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Device type, operating system version, browser type, crash analytics, and application performance metrics to diagnose technical faults and maintain system security.
  • Billing Information: Transaction references, subscription tier identifiers, and license activation tokens. All payments are processed through certified third-party payment gateways (such as Paystack, Apple In-App Purchases, or Google Play Billing). We do not collect or store credit card numbers, debit card security codes, or Mobile Money PINs.

How We Use Information

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate operational and educational purposes:

  • To generate, format, and export curriculum-compliant lesson plans adhering to Ghana Education Service (GES) and National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA) standards.
  • To facilitate internal digital lesson vetting workflows between teachers and headteachers.
  • To provide resilient offline local caching (via browser IndexedDB/localStorage) for teachers working in low-connectivity areas.
  • To verify subscription status, authenticate authorized school accounts, and issue activation licenses.
  • To provide customer support and troubleshoot application issues.
  • To maintain security, prevent unauthorized access, and protect system integrity.

Children’s Privacy

Strict Children's Privacy Commitment: Eduvet School Hub is designed solely for adult educators and school administrative personnel. The Application does not target children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of majority), nor do we knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

All student records entered by educators (such as continuous assessment grades) are handled strictly in an institutional capacity as aggregate or pseudonymized academic records. If an educator or parent becomes aware that personal data of a minor has been submitted inappropriately, please contact us immediately at info@edubyteafrica.com and we will promptly delete the data.

Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose user data to advertisers or commercial data brokers. Information is shared strictly with essential service infrastructure providers:

  • Cloud Hosting & Databases: Google Cloud Platform and Firebase (encrypted storage, authentication, and 99.9% uptime hosting).
  • Payment Gateways: Paystack (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified), Apple App Store, and Google Play for processing subscription payments.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, court order, or official governmental request under the laws of the Republic of Ghana.

Data Storage & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and servers use TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud databases and authentication tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standard.
  • Access Restrictions: Access to backend systems is restricted to authorized technical personnel via multi-factor authentication.

Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain user information for the period necessary to fulfill the educational purposes described in this policy, or until account deletion is requested.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion: Users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

To initiate deletion, send an email to info@edubyteafrica.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 30 business days, following which all personal profile records and lesson notes are permanently erased from our active databases.

User Rights

In accordance with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and international data privacy frameworks, users possess the right to:

  • Access: Review what personal profile and school information is held about them.
  • Rectification: Update or correct inaccurate educator or school details.
  • Portability: Export all generated schemes and lesson notes at any time in Word (.doc), Print/PDF, or JSON formats.
  • Objection / Erasure: Object to processing or request complete account erasure as detailed above.
