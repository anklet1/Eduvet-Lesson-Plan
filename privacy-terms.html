Privacy Policy

Protecting Ghanaian teachers, schools, and educational data under Act 843

Our Core Commitment to Teachers & Schools

Eduvet School Hub is developed and operated by Edubyte Africa Inc. ("Edubyte Africa", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to safeguarding your personal data in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of the Republic of Ghana. This policy governs how we collect, store, process, and protect your information when using the Eduvet Lesson Plan platform.

1 Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly required to generate authentic lesson notes and facilitate school-level academic vetting:

  • Educator Profile Data: Teacher Name, Staff ID, Registered School Name, District, Contact Phone Number, Assigned Class/Level, and Teaching Subjects.
  • Academic Content: Schemes of learning, termly projections, 3-phase lesson plans, TLM selections, and pedagogical reflections created or customized by the teacher.
  • Quality Assurance & Vetting Metadata: Headteacher endorsement stamps, review comments, circuit supervision remarks, and submission timestamps.
  • Billing & License Data: Paystack transaction references, Mobile Money transaction identifiers, and activation code tokens. We do not collect or store credit card details or Mobile Money PINs.
Strict Child & Student Privacy Guarantee: Eduvet is an educator lesson planning and curriculum platform. We do NOT collect, store, or process any personal identification data, names, photos, or biometrics belonging to children or students.

2 How Your Data Is Used

We use the information we gather solely to deliver and optimize educational workflow services:

  • Generating Standards-Based Curriculum lesson plans adhering to NaCCA and GES frameworks.
  • Enabling digital Friday lesson submissions from teachers directly to Headteachers.
  • Providing zero-latency offline browser caching (IndexedDB and localStorage) for seamless operation in low-connectivity areas across Ghana.
  • Validating Pro Tier subscription access and school-wide licenses.
  • Providing technical assistance and customer care via WhatsApp and phone support.

3 Automated AI Generation & Data Security

Our automated AI pedagogical engine operates autonomously to enrich lesson activities with Ghanaian realia, teaching aids, and inquiry prompts.

Only curriculum subject names, strand topics, and instructional indicators are used to synthesize pedagogical activities. No personal teacher information, passwords, or phone numbers are shared with external AI training models.

4 Data Confidentiality & Third Parties

Edubyte Africa Inc. does never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or curriculum notes with third-party advertisers or external data brokers.

Data is shared only with strictly verified infrastructure partners:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: High-availability, encrypted cloud database hosting with 99.99% uptime.
  • Paystack Payments: PCI-DSS certified gateway for secure in-app payments.

5 Your Statutory Rights (Act 843)

Under the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012, you possess the right to:

  • Access & Export: Download and export all your lesson notes, schemes, and vetting logs in Word (.doc), Print/PDF, or JSON formats at any time.
  • Rectification: Modify or correct any personal data, school name, or curriculum subject choices.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of your account and archived vetting notes by contacting our support desk.
